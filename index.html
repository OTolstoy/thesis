Kingdom Financial Stewardship & Voluntary Ministry Participation

Individual Christian Member Questionnaire

Study Area: Selected Churches in Kakamega County, Western Kenya

Purpose: This questionnaire examines the relationship between personal financial health, financial planning behaviour, investment behaviour, debt burden, and Christians' capacity, willingness, and actual participation in voluntary ministry.

Instructions: Please answer each question honestly. There are no right or wrong answers. Do not write your name. The information provided will be treated confidentially and used only for academic purposes.
